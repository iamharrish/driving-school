Frequently Asked Questions

What are the documents required for a learner's license?

You'll typically need proof of age (Aadhar, Birth Certificate), proof of address, and recent passport-size photographs. We assist you in preparing and submitting all required documents.

Do you provide training for both cars and two-wheelers?

Yes, we offer comprehensive training for Light Motor Vehicles (LMV) and gear/non-gear two-wheelers.

Can I choose my training time slots?

Absolutely. We offer slots from 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM to fit your busy schedule.

How long is the typical training course?

Most students complete their basic training in 15-20 sessions, but we adjust the duration based on your progress and comfort level.

Are there female instructors available?

Yes, we have experienced female trainers for women who prefer a female-led learning environment.

Will you help with the driving test at the RTO?

Yes, we provide the vehicle for your test and offer complete assistance during the official RTO driving test.

What is the fee structure for car driving?

Our fees are very competitive and depend on the package you choose. Contact us for a detailed quote.

Is picking up from home/office available?

Yes, we offer doorstep pickup and drop-off services for select areas in Madurai.
